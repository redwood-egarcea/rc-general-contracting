# Phase 2 workshop record

The audience is a Kincardine residential or commercial client checking project fit before making an enquiry. The source supports a combination of design, renovation, new-build, and construction services. The first screen must make that breadth clear and show actual work.

The category's familiar rut is a stock-house banner with a dark overlay and a generic promise. Its predictable opposite is a minimalist luxury-studio page that hides useful detail. Neither is a direction here.

## Grounded systems

The candidate list was established before evaluating the roll. These span spatial presentation, editorial, civic, operational, and signage systems.

| Order | System | Fit and first-surface expression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Architectural presentation board | Clear design and construction images beside an introduction; extends to service indices and contact forms. |
| 2 | Museum room guide | Images with plain captions and clear hierarchy; risks making the company feel like an exhibition. |
| 3 | Builder material sample book | Tactile material-led service groups; available photos do not support a full material catalogue. |
| 4 | Civic tender document | Readable scope and qualification hierarchy; can be too formal for a home enquiry. |
| 5 | Marina wayfinding | A strong local visual reference with clear destinations; risks suggesting marine services not present in the source. |
| 6 | Construction schedule board | Useful sequencing and service labels; must not invent timing or make every service a prescribed step. |
| 7 | Residential architecture magazine | Large authentic room photograph and compact display type; carries company history and services as readable editorial sections. |

Impeccable direction seed `7b1ba3e5`, mode Persuade, assigned candidate 7. The initial sandbox call had no network; the prescribed network-enabled retry returned the same assignment and its catalogue hand. No taste-based re-roll was performed. Candidate 7 became A; candidate 1 is the alternate B.

## Challenger evaluation

Every challenger was evaluated against the assigned direction for audience identification and product clarity. None beat both. The user requested exactly two proposals, so the larger skill-generated tournament is documented here rather than adding six unrelated choices to the approval page.

| Challenger translated to this site | Audience identification | Product clarity | Verdict and discipline retained |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Painted forest planes surrounding a company-work image | A storybook setting would compete with evidence of real work. | Layer travel would delay service and contact discovery. | Declined. Keep a single decisive image plane and a calm text plane; no parallax or invented woodland project. |
| Garden axes and rooms for service categories | Palace and garden references do not match the documented work. | A scenic navigation journey obscures the short service list. | Declined. Keep a strong, uninterrupted visual alignment from heading to work image to next section. |
| Character-catalogue cells for services | Mascot or collectible language does not help a construction enquiry. | Ranked cells imply a service hierarchy unsupported by the source. | Declined. Give every real service a readable name and predictable destination, without rankings or bounce. |
| Botanical folio plates for design and construction stages | Botanical specimens are remote from the work and identity. | A stage narrative could falsely join unrelated project images. | Declined. Keep consistent image captions and scales while stating rendering versus construction explicitly. |
| Marbled pigment field for the hero and controls | Decorative liquid has no connection to the supplied work. | Gesture-led navigation adds a task before the visitor can enquire. | Declined. Commit to one coherent colour system across quiet and active states without a fluid effect. |
| Film cutting rail for project images | Editing-room controls do not match the visitor's goal. | Scrubbing and pinned scraps would hide content and complicate keyboard access. | Declined. Keep image selection and captions deliberate; preserve all material in ordinary document flow. |

These are structural disciplines, not decorative motifs copied from unrelated worlds. Neither proposal uses a catalogue world's artwork. No reference board was selected as the visual authority; the actual company assets and proposed compositions are that authority.

## Shape briefs

**Home `/`:** Persuade. A visitor should identify residential/commercial construction in Kincardine, see approved work, understand the company and services, and reach the enquiry route. Preserve history, working practices, all reviews and rating count, images, social links, and `#contact`. No invented projects or credentials. The chosen direction determines whether finished work or design/construction breadth leads.

**Services `/services/`:** Persuade with reading support. A visitor should find the full service scope in a visible, linked index and decide whether to enquire. Use only source-supported service names and descriptions. Long names wrap; complete descriptions remain open. The route adds a useful reading surface without deleting home content. Desktop side-by-side content becomes a single sequence below 768px.

Both pages share clear navigation, a route to the protected contact form, responsive images, visible focus, reduced motion, and light/dark token systems. Forms and reveal controls carry explicit loading, success, and failure states. Evidence and pending factual questions remain in the audit. No price, completion time, guarantee, review provider, or new case study is implied.

**Selection:** the owner chose A, `rooms-in-focus`, in the comparison page (key `6122fc83`), with no steer and the comp path unchanged. `DESIGN.md` now records that system in the required eight-section format and normative tokens. The original assets and written requirements override generated photo/logo/text discrepancies. Phase 3 may proceed; Phase 5 copy review remains a stop gate.
